Frequently Asked Questions

What topics does Cistern cover?

Cistern focuses on the Water Treatment Grade 1 exam content. The app includes practice questions covering water treatment principles, regulations, equipment, and testing procedures aligned with state certification requirements.

How many practice questions are included?

Cistern v1 includes 150+ original practice questions for the Water Treatment Grade 1 exam. We're continuously expanding our question bank based on updates to EPA guidance and state exam requirements.

Can I access Cistern offline?

Yes! Cistern is designed to work completely offline. Download the app, purchase access, and study anywhere—on the job site, during your commute, or anywhere without internet connection. Your quiz data is never sent to our servers.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Cistern doesn't require an account, login, email, or any personal information. Your progress is saved locally on your device. No account means no data collection and better privacy.

What happens to my progress if I reinstall the app?

Your local quiz history and streak data are stored on your device. If you reinstall Cistern, you'll start fresh with a new score history. We're exploring optional cloud sync in future versions for users who want to preserve their progress across devices.

Is Cistern a study guarantee or a study tool?

Cistern is a study tool to help you prepare. Passing your state certification exam depends on your study effort, background experience, and familiarity with the material. Cistern provides high-quality, source-cited practice questions—but exam success ultimately depends on you.

Do you update questions to match current exam content?

Yes. We monitor EPA guidance and state exam updates, and we review the question bank annually for accuracy. Updates are delivered as free app updates to all users.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You have up to 14 days from purchase to request a refund through your App Store account. After 14 days, Apple's standard refund policy applies. Learn how to request a refund on the App Store.

Will Cistern come to Android?

Yes! We're working on an Android version (v1.1), coming soon. Android users will get the same $12.99 one-time purchase and full offline functionality when it launches.

How do I know the questions are accurate?

Every question in Cistern cites its source—EPA guidance documents, state study guides, or regulatory references. You can verify every answer against the cited source yourself. We manually review every question before release to ensure accuracy.

What if I think a question or answer is wrong?

If you find an error, please email us at support@8086.llc with details. Include the question number, your concern, and the source you're referencing. We take accuracy seriously and will investigate every report.

I have a feature request or feedback.

We'd love to hear from you! Email support@8086.llc with your suggestions. User feedback helps us improve Cistern.
