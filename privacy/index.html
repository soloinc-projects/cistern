Privacy Policy

Cistern doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data about you. Your quiz scores and progress stay on your device. Period.

What Cistern Does Not Collect

  • No personal information: We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identifying details.
  • No accounts: You don't create an account or log in. No username, no password.
  • No quiz answers: We never see what questions you answer or how you answer them.
  • No device identifiers: We don't collect your device ID, IP address, or any unique identifier that could track you across apps.
  • No tracking: No third-party analytics, no IDFA, no cross-app tracking.
  • No location data: Cistern never requests or uses your location.

What Stays On Your Device

The only data Cistern stores is:

  • Quiz score history – Your scores from each quiz you take, stored locally on your device
  • Streak state – How many days in a row you've taken a quiz, stored locally

This data is never transmitted anywhere. It never leaves your device. It's not synced to iCloud, not sent to our servers, not shared with anyone.

How Purchase Information Is Handled

When you buy Cistern:

  • Apple handles your payment – Your credit card, Apple ID, and payment details are managed entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework. Cistern never sees your payment information.
  • We receive a receipt notification – Apple sends us a transaction receipt so we know your purchase was valid. This receipt contains no personal information about you, just a proof of purchase.
  • No recurring charges – Cistern is sold as a non-renewing in-app purchase. You pay once. Your payment method is never charged again.

App Analytics

Cistern sends only aggregate, first-party analytics to understand app health and user engagement:

  • That the app was opened
  • That a quiz was started or completed (no question content, no score details)
  • That a purchase was attempted, completed, or failed

These are all sent to first-party servers only, not to third-party analytics platforms. No personally identifiable information is included.

Children's Privacy

Cistern is designed for professional adults (water treatment operators) and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not intentionally collecting data from children under 13. If a child's parent or guardian becomes aware that a child has provided Cistern with personal information, please contact us immediately.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, used, and shared. Since Cistern collects no personal data, there is nothing to disclose, delete, or opt out of. You have nothing to request.

Data Deletion

Since Cistern stores no account data, no personal information, and no data linked to your identity, there is no account to delete and no personal data we hold to delete. All data Cistern knows about is stored on your device only and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Offline Operation

Cistern works entirely offline. You can study on an airplane, in a basement, or anywhere with zero network connectivity. Cistern makes no network calls except during the initial in-app purchase flow, which Apple handles.

Third-Party Services

Cistern does not use any third-party SDKs or services that collect data. No Firebase, no Mixpanel, no advertising networks. Your quiz data never touches a third-party server.

Security

Your device's OS provides security for locally stored data. The iOS operating system encrypts app data at rest. Cistern does not implement custom encryption because it has no reason to: we don't store sensitive data, and the OS provides encryption by default.

Privacy Policy Changes

If Cistern's privacy practices change, we will update this policy and notify you within the app. Major changes will require your consent before taking effect.

Contact Us

If you have any privacy concerns or questions, email us at support@8086.llc.
